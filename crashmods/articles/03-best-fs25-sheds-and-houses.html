Best FS25 Sheds and Farmhouses: 16 Placeable Mods Compared by Price and Upkeep

The base game gives Farming Simulator 25 a decent set of buildings, but most yards end up with the same few sheds. Placeable mods fix that, and they differ more than the screenshots suggest. A $10,000 shed that costs $1 a day plays very differently from a $370,000 hall with a built-in workshop. Below are 16 sheds, workshops and farmhouses from the CrashMods library, grouped by what they do and compared on price and daily upkeep. All of them are free to download.

Cheap sheds for a new farm

Metal Open Shed: $10,000

The cheapest useful building here. The Metal Open Shed keeps vehicles under cover and also stores crops. It has AutoLoad for bales, BigBags and pallets, and costs just $1 a day. You can color it to match the rest of the farm.

Small Hall: $25,000

The Small Hall is sized for a few machines and fits a tight yard. It has a single roller door for everyday use and a double roller door for bigger machines. Interior lighting is included, and upkeep is $1 a day.

North American Shed Pack: from $25,000

Two shed styles you see on farms across North America. The North American Shed Pack has a Quonset for $25,000, a version with doors on both ends for $27,500, and a pole shed for $32,500. The pole shed comes in six trim colors, eight store items in total. A good fit for US maps like Riverbend Springs.

Big storage for machines and grain

Big Garage: $35,000

At 44 by 12 meters, the Big Garage parks a long row of machinery under one roof for $10 a day. It works on any map and in multiplayer.

Big Storage: $50,000

Big Storage takes crops and whole vehicles, has shelves for pallets, and one big door plus six smaller doors. Outside lights switch on by themselves at night. An optional solar panel configuration earns money. Upkeep is only $1 a day, which makes it one of the best value buildings on this list.

WS11 Machinery Shed: $65,000

An 11 by 20 meter shed in two versions, with the side door on the left or on the right, so it fits your yard layout. The WS11 Machinery Shed has indoor lights on a switch box and an outdoor light that turns on at night. Upkeep is $25 a day.

Wide Garage: $80,000

The Wide Garage has a drive-through passage and stores both machines and grain for $2 a day. A solar panel option costs another $40,000 and brings in $80 per hour.

Workshops and multi-purpose halls

System Hall: $150,000

Modelled on a real Hörmann hall from 2009. The System Hall has four gates and three bulk bays separated by concrete walls, and the rest of the floor takes machines, bulk goods or bales. Upkeep is $50 a day.

American Midwest Truck Shop: $150,000

One building, several jobs. The American Midwest Truck Shop stores crops and machines, repairs and customizes vehicles, works as a selling point, and has a 35,000 liter diesel tank. Every roller door and man door can be used. Upkeep is $10 a day.

Machine Hall: $370,000

The most expensive building here, built for a large fleet. The Machine Hall has a workshop right next to the hall, so a broken machine can be serviced on the spot instead of driven across the map. It costs $50 a day and works in multiplayer.

Farmhouses

A-Frame Cabin: $35,500

A rustic wooden cabin with large windows and a steep A-frame roof. The A-Frame Cabin can be your main residence, a hunting lodge or a guest house. Note the $100 daily upkeep, which is high for its price.

Modern House: $60,000

A clean, contemporary farmhouse for $10 a day. Modern House has a living room with an open kitchen, two bedrooms, an office, a bathroom and a boiler room, with a choice of wall colors.

Small Barndominium: $100,000

Living space and a two-car garage under one roof. The Small Barndominium has a big living area with a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. You can finish the walls in metal, wood or brick. Upkeep is $10 a day.

Big Modern House: $120,000

The family-sized option. Big Modern House has three bedrooms plus a master bedroom, a master bathroom, an office and a garage for two cars. Upkeep is $15 a day.

BarnDom: $125,000

A home and a workshop in one building. BarnDom has three bedrooms and an open living room with a fireplace, plus a built-in shop where you can repair, customize, repaint and wash vehicles. Upkeep is $15 a day.

For atmosphere: Very Old Timberframe Barn

Not the most practical choice, and that is the point. The Very Old Timberframe Barn recreates a barn from the early 1800s, complete with an old pigsty in the middle. Vehicles can drive in, but only very small ones reach the corners. It costs $45,420 plus $120 a day, so it suits roleplay and historic farms more than a profit run.

Which one should you pick?

  • Starting out: Metal Open Shed or Small Hall. Both cost $1 a day.
  • Best value storage: Big Storage, with pallet shelves, solar income and $1 upkeep.
  • Repairs without driving across the map: American Midwest Truck Shop, or Machine Hall for a big fleet.
  • A farmhouse that also does work: BarnDom.

To install any of these on Windows, put the downloaded .zip file, without extracting it, into Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods, then activate the mod when you load your save.
